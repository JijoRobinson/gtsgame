Contact Us

Questions, bug reports, feature ideas, or partnership requests — we'd love to hear from you.

Email

The fastest way to reach us is by email: hello@guessthesecond.com

We aim to reply within 2–3 business days.

What to include

  • Bug reports: what happened, what you expected, your device/browser, and the game mode (Stopwatch, Type & Guess, Versus, or Teams).
  • Feature ideas: tell us how you'd use it — a quick story helps more than a one-line request.
  • Partnerships / press: a short description of your organization and what you have in mind.

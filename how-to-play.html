How to Play Guess the Time

Guess the Time is a free browser game that tests your sense of time. A hidden timer runs, and you guess how long it lasted. Here's everything you need to know — pick it up in under a minute.

The basic idea

Every round has a secret duration. The timer starts with a 3–2–1 countdown, then runs without showing you the elapsed time. When it stops, you enter your guess in seconds (decimals allowed, like 7.4). The closer your guess, the better your score.

Game modes

⏱️ Stopwatch (solo)

The target is fixed at 10 seconds. You click Start, then click Stop when you think 10 seconds have passed. You'll lose 10 points for every 0.1 seconds you're off, for a score out of 100.

⌨️ Type & Guess (solo)

The timer runs for a random 3–12 seconds and stops on its own. You then type your estimate. This is the best mode for genuinely improving your internal clock, because you can't rely on a memorized rhythm.

👥 Versus (2–10 players)

Enter each player's name, start the round, and pass the device around. Each player privately types a guess after the timer stops. You'll see a ranked leaderboard; the closest player wins. Ties are supported.

🏆 Teams (up to 10 teams × 10 players)

Set up team names and rosters, then start. Every player on every team gets one private guess. Each team's score is its members' average absolute error; the lowest average wins. Player order is shuffled so teammates don't guess back-to-back.

Scoring

  • Stopwatch: score out of 100, minus 10 points per 0.1 second of error.
  • Type & Guess / Versus / Teams: ranked by absolute error (how far your guess is from the real time, in either direction). Lower is better.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. The game runs entirely in your browser. No signup, login, or personal information is required.

Does it work on phones?

Yes — the game is fully responsive and works on any modern phone, tablet, or desktop browser.

How is the random time chosen?

Each Type, Versus, or Teams round picks a new time uniformly at random between 3 and 12 seconds, shown to the hundredth of a second.

Can more than 10 people play?

Versus supports up to 10 individual players. Teams mode supports up to 100 total players (10 teams of 10). For larger groups, just split into more teams or play multiple rounds.

Is the game really free?

Yes. The site is supported by advertisements; gameplay is completely free with no paywalls.

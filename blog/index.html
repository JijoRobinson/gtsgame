Why we write about time

Your sense of time is one of the most taken-for-granted skills you own. Musicians use it for rhythm, athletes for pacing, cooks for doneness, teachers for classroom transitions — and everyone for showing up on time. Yet most people are surprisingly bad at estimating even ten seconds without a clock.

Our short, practical articles show you how to test and improve your time perception, along with creative ways to use timing games at parties, in classrooms, and during team-building sessions.

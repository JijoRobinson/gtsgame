Why Play Guess the Second?

Guess the Second is a simple but surprisingly addictive browser game that tests your sense of time. Whether you have 30 seconds to kill or you're hosting a game night with friends, it delivers instant fun with zero setup.

Unlike reaction games that only test speed, Guess the Second challenges your internal clock — the mental ability to estimate durations without a visible reference. It's a skill used in music, cooking, sports, and everyday planning. Best of all, it improves with practice.

Four ways to play

  • Stopwatch — try to stop a timer at exactly 10 seconds.
  • Type & Guess — a random hidden interval; type your estimate.
  • Versus — 2 to 10 players compete on one device.
  • Teams — multiple teams, each with multiple members.

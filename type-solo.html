About Type & Guess

This mode is the purest test of your time perception. Because the target is random each round (between 3 and 12 seconds), you can't simply memorize a rhythm — you have to genuinely feel the passing seconds.

Tips for a better guess

  • Silently count "one one-thousand, two one-thousand..." for a rough anchor.
  • Pay attention to your breathing — a relaxed breath takes about 4 seconds.
  • Try multiple rounds; most players improve within minutes.

Want to make it competitive? Challenge friends in Versus mode or organize team play.

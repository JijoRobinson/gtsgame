How Versus mode works

Versus is a pass-and-play party game that runs on a single phone, tablet, or computer. The same hidden timer runs once; then each player types their guess privately so nobody can copy. After the last player, you see a ranked leaderboard — the player closest to the actual time wins.

  • Supports 2 to 10 players per round.
  • Each round picks a new random time between 3 and 12 seconds.
  • Ties are possible and shown explicitly on the results screen.

Have larger groups? Try Teams mode, which supports up to 10 teams of up to 10 members each.

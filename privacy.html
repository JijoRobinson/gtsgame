Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This policy explains what information Guess the Time collects and how it is used.

Overview

Guess the Time is a free, browser-based game. You do not need to create an account, and we do not ask for personally identifying information to play. Gameplay (player names you enter, guesses, and scores) is processed locally in your browser and is not sent to or stored on our servers.

Advertising

This site is supported by advertising. We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit. These companies may use cookies and similar technologies to measure ad performance and to personalize the ads you see, including using information about your visits to this and other websites.

You can learn more about how Google uses data by visiting Google's partner sites policy. You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings, or through aboutads.info (EU users: youronlinechoices.eu).

Cookies & local storage

Third-party advertisers may set and read cookies on your browser. Our game itself does not use tracking cookies. If we add best-score persistence in the future, it will be stored in your browser's local storage and remain on your device only.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregated analytics to understand traffic and improve the site (for example, total page views and popular game modes). These reports do not identify individual users.

Children's privacy

The game is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices (GDPR / CCPA)

Visitors in the EU/EEA and California have rights under the GDPR and CCPA, including access, correction, and deletion of personal data. Because we do not maintain accounts or store personal gameplay data on our servers, most of these rights can be exercised directly through your browser's cookie settings or by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email us at hello@guessthesecond.com.

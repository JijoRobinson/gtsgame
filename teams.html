How Teams mode works

Teams mode is ideal for classrooms, office games, or large parties. Set up each team's name and roster, then start. The game runs one round per player position: in Round 1, Player 1 of every team watches the same hidden timer and types a guess; after all their answers, the round's winner is announced. In Round 2, Player 2 of every team gets a fresh hidden time — and so on until every player has competed.

At the end, each team's score is its members' average absolute error across all rounds. The team with the lowest average wins — so even if one teammate is way off, a strong performance from the rest can still carry the team.

  • Up to 10 teams and 10 players per team (up to 100 total players).
  • Each round, the same-position player from every team competes on the same hidden time.
  • Round results and live standings are shown before moving to the next round.
  • Full per-team breakdown is shown on the final results screen.
